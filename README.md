## About Me

I'm a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate and researcher at **Universitas Muhammadiyah Yogyakarta (UMY), Indonesia**, with a strong interest in mechanical engineering, scientific research, machine learning, engineering simulation, and computational analysis.

My academic research focuses on applying data-driven approaches and intelligent computational methods to mechanical engineering problems, particularly manufacturing processes, predictive modelling, and vibration-based structural condition monitoring.

I participated in an international student exchange programme at **Chang Gung University, Taiwan**, expanding my academic knowledge and international experience.

Currently, I'm undertaking an engineering internship at **PT Kilang Pertamina Internasional, Refinery Unit V Balikpapan**, gaining practical exposure to industrial engineering practices and refinery operations.

I'm committed to continuous learning, scientific innovation, and integrating theoretical knowledge with real-world engineering applications.
